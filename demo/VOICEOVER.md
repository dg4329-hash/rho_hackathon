Matches `demo/index.html`: 6 scenes × 20 s. ~290 words at a calm launch-ad pace (~145 wpm).

**Suggested settings:**
- **Model:** Eleven Multilingual v2 (or v3).
- **Voice:** a warm, confident narrator (e.g. "Adam", "Brian" or "Rachel").
- **Voice settings:** Stability 45–55 %, Similarity 75 %, Style 15–25 %, Speaker boost on.
- **Pacing:** the `<break time="…" />` tags keep each paragraph inside its 20-second scene. If a line runs long, trim words rather than speeding up the voice.

Paste everything between the lines into ElevenLabs as one generation. The scene headings are for you; don't paste them.

## Sync tips
- **Aligning audio:** export the audio as MP3 and start it the moment you press space (the cover fades in about 0.9 s), or drop it onto the recorded MP4 in any editor. Each paragraph starts on a scene boundary: 0:00, 0:20, 0:40, 1:00, 1:20, 1:40.
- **If the voice runs long:** remove the least important sentence in that paragraph. Don't retime the film.
- **Handoff from the live talk:** if the presenter says "Here's mesh in two minutes," the film can start silent on the cover and the voice begins with Scene 1.
